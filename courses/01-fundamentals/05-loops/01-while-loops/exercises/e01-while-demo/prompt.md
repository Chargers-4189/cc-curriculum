Follow along with the lesson in `While.java`. Each section of the lesson has a matching `// --- Section ---` comment in the file.

1. Print "Welcome to Java!" 100 times with a `while` loop.
2. Add up the numbers 1 through 9.
3. Remove the `i++` to make an infinite loop. Stop it with **Ctrl + C**, then put the `i++` back.
4. Write the sentinel loop that adds numbers until the user enters 0.
5. Rewrite the sentinel loop as a `do-while`.
