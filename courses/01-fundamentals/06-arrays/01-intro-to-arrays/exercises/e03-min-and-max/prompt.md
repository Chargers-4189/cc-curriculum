difficulty: medium

Read **5** whole numbers into an array, then print the smallest and the largest.

```
Enter 5 numbers: 7 2 9 4 6
Min: 2
Max: 9
```

**Hint:** start `min` and `max` at the **first element**, not at 0. Why would 0 be a bad starting value? (Try the test with all negative numbers.)
