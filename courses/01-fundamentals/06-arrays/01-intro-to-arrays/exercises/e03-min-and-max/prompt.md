---
title: "Min and Max"
type: practice
difficulty: medium
---

Read **5** whole numbers into an array, then print the smallest and the largest.

```
Enter 5 numbers: 7 2 9 4 6
Min: 2
Max: 9
```

**Hint:** start `min` and `max` at the **first element**. Starting at 0 fails when every number is negative.

## In Labs

Download it, write your code in `Main.java`, and check it:

```bash
ccm get min-and-max
cd min-and-max
ccm test
```
